How the deer move between the two cameras

Each line follows one deer frame by frame at its feet: a dot where it steps into view, an arrow where it leaves. 6 of the 10 visits were caught by both cameras. The images below put the two views side by side, with a dashed line from where the deer left one picture to where it showed up in the other, and the seconds in between.
